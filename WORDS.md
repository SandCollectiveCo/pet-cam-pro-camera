# The camera page's words

Every word the page shows is in `src/words.js`, and every one is picked:

- the plan's own, on 2026-11-19 (`docs/WEB-CAMERA.md` § 1, § 4 and § 5);
- the apps' own words, where the page says the same thing an app does;
- the rest on 2026-11-24, each from two or three drafts, the microphone's line among them. The drafts each was picked from are in this file at commit `97e9812`.

A word added after this is a draft until it's picked too: put it here with two or three drafts and a pick.

Pictures of every state, with these words in place: `npm run shots`, then `.tmp/shots/sheet.png`.

The page keeps to the apps' rules for copy: a label says what it is, with nothing under it; it says what happens to which device ("this phone", never "it"); errors are plain and short. It never says "home" or "account": the page knows neither, only "the viewer" that started it. "Phone" below is whatever the device is: a tablet, a computer, and "this Mac", "this iPhone" where the page can tell.
